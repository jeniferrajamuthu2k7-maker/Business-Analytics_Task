# Week 13 – E-Commerce Sales and Business Performance Analysis

## Objective
To analyze an E-Commerce dataset using Tableau Public, identify meaningful business insights, explore sales patterns and trends, and create an interactive dashboard for better business decision-making.

## Dataset Details
- Dataset: Global E-Commerce Sales Dataset
- Records: 2,000
- Tool: Tableau Public
- File Format: CSV

## Tools Used
- Tableau Public
- Microsoft Excel / CSV
- GitHub

## Dashboard Visualizations

1. **Sales by Product Category – Bar Chart**
   - Compares sales performance across different product categories.

2. **Monthly Sales Trend – Line Chart**
   - Shows monthly sales patterns and changes over time.

3. **Sales vs Profit – Scatter Plot**
   - Analyzes the relationship between sales and profit.

4. **Product Sales Contribution – Treemap**
   - Displays product-wise sales contribution.

## Interactive Filters
- Region
- Product Category
- Payment Method

These filters allow users to explore the dashboard and compare different business categories.

## Key Findings
- Sales performance can be compared across different product categories.
- Monthly sales trends help identify high and low sales periods.
- The scatter plot helps explore the relationship between sales and profit.
- The treemap highlights the contribution of individual products to overall sales.

## Suggestions
- Improve marketing strategies for low-performing product categories.
- Analyze monthly sales trends to plan seasonal promotions.
- Monitor products that generate lower or negative profits.

## Recommendations
- Maintain sufficient inventory for high-performing products.
- Use regional and category-wise sales analysis to improve business strategies.
- Review pricing and discount strategies to improve profitability.

## Conclusion
The E-Commerce Sales Analysis dashboard provides an interactive view of sales, profit, product categories, and business performance. It helps identify important patterns and supports data-driven business decisions.
